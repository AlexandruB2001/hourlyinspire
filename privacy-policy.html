HourlyInspire Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

In short: HourlyInspire does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. HourlyInspire has no account or sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The app does not send your personal information to us or to anyone else. The only network request it makes is the update check described below.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few things locally on your iPhone: your reminder settings (times, frequency and daily maximum), your chosen theme, the quotes you mark as favourites, which quotes you have already seen, your daily goals (including any you write yourself), and which goals you completed on each day for up to 120 days, used for your streak and monthly history. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Widget

If you add the HourlyInspire widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, the app passes it upcoming quotes through a storage area on your iPhone that only the app and its widget can read. Nothing is sent off your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, HourlyInspire schedules reminders directly on your device using Apple's local notification system. No push server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the app, or turn off notifications at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Sharing

When you choose to share a quote, the iOS share sheet opens and you decide where it goes. HourlyInspire does not see or keep what you share.

App updates

When it opens, the app may check Expo's update service (expo.dev) for new versions of its content and design. To deliver the right update, this check sends basic technical information such as the app version, the platform (iOS) and a random identifier created for this installation. It does not include your name, contact details, settings, favourites or anything else you do in the app, and it is used only to deliver updates. See Expo's privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy.

Children

HourlyInspire does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
