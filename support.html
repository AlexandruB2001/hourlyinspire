I'm not getting reminders

Open your iPhone's Settings > Notifications > HourlyInspire and make sure Allow Notifications is on. In the app, open Settings and check that reminders are on and your first and last reminder times cover the current hour. Tap "Send a test reminder" to check: it arrives in about 5 seconds. Focus modes can also hide reminders.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row on which you completed every one of your goals (100%). Today joins the streak as soon as all of today's goals are ticked. An unfinished today doesn't break your streak until the day is over.

I forgot to tick my goals yesterday

Open the Goals tab and use the arrows at the top to go back to that day, then tick what you did. You can update any day from the last 120 days.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, search for HourlyInspire and choose "Hourly Quote". For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize > Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and add HourlyInspire.

Will I lose my goals if I change phones?

Your goals, history and favourites are stored only on your iPhone, so nothing is sent to a server. They move to a new iPhone if you transfer your data with an iPhone backup or Quick Start. Deleting the app deletes them.
